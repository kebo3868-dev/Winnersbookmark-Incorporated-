# Restaurant Prospect Criteria — Winners Bookmark Agency

Target: **independent** restaurants in St. Petersburg / Tampa Bay / nearby FL cities. Skip chains and franchises (no local decision-maker).

## The 20-point friction checklist
Score a restaurant higher when you see more of these:
1. Weak or dated website
2. Old / hard-to-read menu
3. Broken or clunky online ordering
4. No reservation/booking system
5. Poor mobile experience
6. Slow-loading pages
7. Low or declining Google rating
8. Owner doesn't respond to reviews
9. Confusing or wrong hours listed
10. No clear call-to-action on the site
11. No catering / large-order lead capture
12. No SMS follow-up after a visit
13. No email capture
14. No loyalty / repeat-customer system
15. Missed-call risk (single line, busy kitchen)
16. Negative reviews mentioning "no one answered the phone"
17. Negative reviews mentioning wait/communication
18. Active but neglected Instagram (DMs likely unread)
19. Third-party-only ordering (paying 20–30% to apps)
20. No way to ask a quick question without calling

## Priority scoring guide
- **9–10:** Obvious pain (e.g., reviews complaining no one answers) + reachable owner + decent volume.
- **6–8:** Clear friction, owner findable, worth a personalized message.
- **3–5:** Some friction but harder to reach or lower volume — batch later.
- **1–2:** Chain-like, no friction, or no contact path — skip.
